The Sydney AI Safety Space is a volunteer run and donation-supported coworking space based at Broad Church, 3 Little Queen St, Chippendale.

We provide free office space for people working in the field of AI safety (including policy work, technical research, field building, personal study, or engineering projects).

Visit Us!

Come cowork with us for free! Anyone interested in AI safety can drop by as a guest, for lunch or a day of coworking. You're welcome to keep coming back as often as you like. Just email to let us know you're coming, and we'll add you to the Slack (shared with Australian EAs) to introduce yourself to the community.

When you arrive, push open the door (or knock if it's locked), and come up the stairs to the coworking area!

Our space operates on a hot desking model, with an average occupancy of around five members:

  • No permanently assigned desks - choose any available workspace when you arrive.
  • We have meeting rooms, standing desks, and collaborative areas.
  • Clear your workspace at the end of each day.
  • Booking is optional, otherwise it's first-come, first-served for desk space, with priority access for members.
