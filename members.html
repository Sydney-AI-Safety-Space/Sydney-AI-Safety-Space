Become a Member

Coming in regularly and working on AI safety? You're a member! Membership involves:

  • Priority access to desks.
  • Committing to volunteer for the space (help with events, maintenance, etc).
  • Sharing a brief quarterly update on your work.

We have a limited number of physical keys for after hours access, which are distributed among a subset of members.
